# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Inside Sales Copilot turns a pasted meeting invite into a cited discovery brief for a presales discovery call. Account executives, sales engineers (SEs) and solution architects at IT services companies such as Royal Cyber spend hours researching a prospect by hand before each call. The research is spread across browser tabs, can pick the wrong person, and has no standard format.

The SE pastes an invite. The Copilot researches the company from public web sources, checks each attendee, matches case studies from a snapshot of the public Royal Cyber website, and drafts a brief. The SE edits and approves the brief. Approval locks the brief and enables a Markdown export.

Source of truth for scope: `docs/brd-inside-sales-copilot.md`. Where the one-pager and the draft plan differ, the brief follows the draft plan. The architecture document and the draft plan are used only as context. Items they add that the brief does not contain are listed under "Ambiguities and Open Questions" and are not turned into requirements.

Out of scope (roadmap): learning rules from SE edits, Outlook integration, case study management screens, full role-based access control (including the two-demo-user BU filter and Cognito), an RFP response copilot, and any feature not listed here.

Workspace rules that apply: synthetic data only for any attendee or customer data in tests and fixtures, AWS work in us-east-1 only, and no more than one Amazon Bedrock request per second.

## Glossary

- **Copilot**: The Inside Sales Copilot system described in this document.
- **SE**: Sales engineer. The main user, who pastes the invite and edits and approves the brief.
- **Invite**: Meeting invite text pasted by the SE. It names a company and lists attendees with names and titles.
- **Invite_Parser**: The Copilot component that turns Invite text into a company and an attendee list.
- **Attendee**: A person listed in the Invite.
- **Company_Researcher**: The Copilot component that researches the prospect company from public web sources.
- **Fact**: A statement about the company (industry, size tier, news item, or pain point) shown in a Brief.
- **Source_Link**: A URL, with the page title, of the public web page that supports a Fact or an Attendee status.
- **Size_Tier**: One of four values: enterprise, mid-market, SMB, boutique.
- **Attendee_Verifier**: The Copilot component that checks each Attendee using public information only.
- **Verified**: Attendee status that requires both the employer and the title found in public information to match the Invite.
- **Unverified**: Attendee status for every Attendee that is not Verified. It carries a stated reason.
- **Snapshot**: A pre-scraped copy of the public Royal Cyber case study pages, held locally, with each record tagged by BU.
- **BU**: Royal Cyber business unit. The BU list is decided after the Snapshot is scraped.
- **Case_Study_Matcher**: The Copilot component that selects case studies from the Snapshot.
- **Brief**: The discovery brief produced by the Copilot. It contains an agenda, questions to ask, pain-point hypotheses and case studies to mention, together with the company snapshot and the attendee list.
- **Brief_Generator**: The Copilot component that drafts the Brief.
- **Draft**: Brief status before approval. A Draft can be edited.
- **Approved**: Brief status after the SE approves. An Approved Brief is locked.
- **Section**: A named part of the Brief that the SE can edit inline.
- **Original_Text**: The text of a Section as generated by the Copilot.
- **Edited_Text**: The text of a Section after the SE changes it.
- **Markdown_Export**: A Markdown file of an Approved Brief that includes every Source_Link.
- **PII_Masker**: The Copilot mechanism that masks personally identifiable information (PII), such as email addresses, using Amazon Bedrock Guardrails.
- **Model_Gateway**: The single Copilot component through which every Amazon Bedrock request passes.
- **Flagged_Section**: A Section marked in the Brief as not produced because its research step failed.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Produce a draft brief from a pasted invite

**User Story:** As an SE, I want to paste a meeting invite and receive a draft brief, so that I can prepare for a discovery call without researching by hand.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the SE submits Invite text containing 1 to 10,000 characters that names a company, THE Copilot SHALL produce a Draft Brief for that company and display it to the SE.
2. THE Copilot SHALL read the company name only from the Invite text entered at runtime, with no company name hard-coded in the Copilot.
3. WHEN a Brief is produced, THE Brief_Generator SHALL include in the Brief an agenda, questions to ask, pain-point hypotheses, and case studies to mention, with each of the four sections containing at least 1 item.
4. WHEN a Brief is produced, THE Copilot SHALL set the Brief status to Draft.
5. IF a field is missing from the Invite, THEN THE Invite_Parser SHALL record that field as empty and SHALL NOT supply a value for it.
6. IF the Invite text names no company, THEN THE Copilot SHALL display a message indicating that the company could not be determined, SHALL NOT produce a Brief, and SHALL retain the Invite text the SE entered. (See Ambiguity A1.)
7. IF the submitted Invite text is empty or contains only whitespace, THEN THE Copilot SHALL display a message indicating that Invite text is required, SHALL NOT produce a Brief, and SHALL retain the Invite text the SE entered.
8. IF the Invite text exceeds 10,000 characters, THEN THE Copilot SHALL display a message indicating that the Invite is too long, SHALL NOT produce a Brief, and SHALL retain the Invite text the SE entered.

### Requirement 2: Cite every fact

**User Story:** As an SE, I want every fact in the brief to have a source link, so that I can check it before I use it on a call.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN THE Company_Researcher produces a Fact, THE Company_Researcher SHALL attach at least one and at most 3 Source_Links to that Fact, where each Source_Link is a complete URL that begins with "http://" or "https://" and points to the public web page the Fact was taken from.
2. IF a Fact has no Source_Link, THEN THE Copilot SHALL omit that Fact from the Brief and SHALL NOT display any placeholder in its place.
3. WHEN THE Copilot displays a Fact in the Brief, THE Copilot SHALL display each Source_Link of that Fact on the same line or in the same list item as that Fact, as a link that the SE can open.
4. WHEN THE Company_Researcher obtains a Fact, THE Company_Researcher SHALL take it only from web pages that are accessible without login, payment, or any other credentials.
5. IF every Fact for a Company is omitted under criterion 2, THEN THE Copilot SHALL display a Brief that states that no sourced Facts were found for that Company.

### Requirement 3: Assign a company size tier

**User Story:** As an SE, I want the company classified by size, so that I can pitch at the right level.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Company_Researcher finds an employee count for the company that is supported by a Source_Link, THE Company_Researcher SHALL assign exactly one Size_Tier to the company in the Brief.
2. THE Company_Researcher SHALL assign the Size_Tier from the employee count as follows: enterprise for more than 5,000 employees, mid-market for 500 to 5,000 employees, SMB for 50 to 499 employees, and boutique for 1 to 49 employees. (See Ambiguity A2.)
3. WHEN the Brief is displayed, THE Copilot SHALL show the Size_Tier in the company snapshot together with at least one Source_Link that supports the employee count.
4. IF the Company_Researcher finds no employee count supported by a Source_Link, THEN THE Company_Researcher SHALL assign no Size_Tier and THE Copilot SHALL show an indicator in the company snapshot that the size was not found, while still showing the rest of the Brief.

### Requirement 4: Verify attendees without guessing

**User Story:** As an SE, I want each attendee checked against public information, so that I do not prepare using the wrong person's profile.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Attendee_Verifier SHALL assign each Attendee exactly one status, Verified or Unverified, and SHALL NOT leave any Attendee in the Brief without a status.
2. WHEN public information contains a page whose employer and title both match the Invite entry for the Attendee, THE Attendee_Verifier SHALL assign the status Verified and SHALL store the Source_Link of that page. A match means the employer name and the title are equal after ignoring letter case, leading and trailing whitespace, and punctuation.
3. IF the employer, the title, or both do not match, or no public information is found, THEN THE Attendee_Verifier SHALL assign the status Unverified and SHALL NOT store a Source_Link for that Attendee.
4. IF the Attendee_Verifier cannot complete the search for an Attendee within 10 seconds, or the search returns an error, THEN THE Attendee_Verifier SHALL assign the status Unverified with a reason indicating the search failed, and SHALL continue verifying the remaining Attendees.
5. WHEN the Attendee_Verifier assigns the status Unverified, THE Attendee_Verifier SHALL state exactly one reason from this set: no public information found, employer does not match, title does not match, employer and title do not match, or search failed.
6. WHEN public information for an Attendee returns more than one page that matches the employer and title, THE Attendee_Verifier SHALL store the Source_Link of exactly one of those pages.
7. THE Attendee_Verifier SHALL use public information only, and SHALL NOT use content that requires a login or payment to view.
8. WHEN the Copilot generates the Brief, THE Copilot SHALL display for each Attendee the status, and the Source_Link if the status is Verified or the reason if the status is Unverified.
9. THE Copilot SHALL send every attendee search request through one function, so that a guardrail can be added in that function without changes elsewhere. (See Ambiguity A3.)

### Requirement 5: Match case studies from the Royal Cyber snapshot

**User Story:** As an SE, I want relevant Royal Cyber case studies suggested with a reason, so that I can mention them on the call.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the SE requests a Brief, THE Case_Study_Matcher SHALL select up to 3 case studies, and every selected case study SHALL exist in the Snapshot.
2. WHEN the Case_Study_Matcher selects a case study, THE Case_Study_Matcher SHALL state one reason of 1 to 300 characters that names the attribute of the Company or call (industry, size tier, or pain point) the case study matches.
3. WHERE the SE chooses a BU, THE Case_Study_Matcher SHALL select only case studies tagged with that BU.
4. WHERE the SE chooses no BU, THE Case_Study_Matcher SHALL select from case studies of all BUs.
5. IF the Snapshot holds fewer than 3 matching case studies for the chosen BU, THEN THE Case_Study_Matcher SHALL return only the matching ones, SHALL NOT fill the remaining places with case studies from another BU, and THE Copilot SHALL show a message in the Brief indicating that fewer than 3 matches were found (or none, when the count is 0).
6. WHEN the Snapshot is built, THE Copilot SHALL obtain case study content by scraping the public Royal Cyber website directly, and SHALL NOT use web search tool results as Snapshot content.
7. WHEN a case study is selected, THE Copilot SHALL display that case study's Source_Link in the Brief, and the Source_Link SHALL be the URL of the Royal Cyber page the case study was scraped from. (See Ambiguity A4 for the number of case studies and for the BU list.)

### Requirement 6: Edit the brief inline

**User Story:** As an SE, I want to edit each section of the draft brief, so that I can correct and improve it before the call.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE a Brief is Draft, THE Copilot SHALL provide an inline edit control on each Section of that Brief.
2. WHEN the SE activates the edit control of a Section, THE Copilot SHALL show the current text of that Section (the Edited_Text if one exists, otherwise the Original_Text) in an editable field within that Section, and SHALL leave all other Sections unchanged.
3. WHEN the SE saves an edit to a Section, THE Copilot SHALL store the submitted text as the Edited_Text, SHALL keep the Original_Text of that Section unchanged, and SHALL display the Edited_Text in the Section within 1 second.
4. WHEN the SE cancels an edit to a Section, THE Copilot SHALL discard the unsaved text and SHALL display the Section text as it was before the edit began.
5. IF the SE saves an edit whose text is empty or contains only whitespace, THEN THE Copilot SHALL reject the save, SHALL display an error message indicating that the Section cannot be empty, and SHALL keep the previously stored Edited_Text and Original_Text unchanged.
6. IF a save of an edit fails, THEN THE Copilot SHALL display an error message indicating that the edit was not saved, SHALL keep the SE's unsaved text in the editable field, and SHALL keep the previously stored Edited_Text and Original_Text unchanged.
7. THE Copilot SHALL keep both the Original_Text and the Edited_Text of each edited Section, so that edit history is available to a later feature.
8. WHILE a Brief is not Draft, THE Copilot SHALL NOT provide an inline edit control on any Section of that Brief.

### Requirement 7: Approve and lock the brief

**User Story:** As an SE, I want to approve the brief so that it is locked as the version I will use.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the SE approves a Draft Brief, THE Copilot SHALL set the Brief status to Approved and SHALL keep the Original_Text and the Edited_Text of every Section exactly as they were at the moment of approval.
2. WHILE a Brief is Approved, THE Copilot SHALL reject every request to edit any Section of that Brief, including requests to change, clear or replace Section text.
3. IF the SE requests an edit to an Approved Brief, THEN THE Copilot SHALL show a message indicating that the Brief is approved and locked, and SHALL leave the Original_Text, the Edited_Text and the status of every Section unchanged.
4. IF the approval of a Draft Brief cannot be completed, THEN THE Copilot SHALL show an error message indicating that approval failed, and SHALL keep the Brief status as Draft with every Section unchanged.
5. THE Copilot SHALL provide no action that returns an Approved Brief to Draft. (See Ambiguity A5.)

### Requirement 8: Export the approved brief

**User Story:** As an SE, I want to download the approved brief as Markdown with its source links, so that I can share it and keep it with the opportunity record.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE a Brief is Approved, THE Copilot SHALL provide a Markdown_Export control that is enabled for that Brief, and WHEN the SE activates it, THE Copilot SHALL download one Markdown file containing the whole Brief within 5 seconds.
2. WHILE a Brief is Draft, THE Copilot SHALL disable or omit the Markdown_Export control, and THE Copilot SHALL NOT produce a Markdown file for that Brief by any user action.
3. THE Markdown_Export SHALL include every Source_Link that appears in the Brief, each as a Markdown link that shows its URL and is placed in the same Section as the fact it supports.
4. THE Markdown_Export SHALL contain the Edited_Text of each edited Section and the Original_Text of each unedited Section, and SHALL contain exactly one version of each Section's text. (See Ambiguity A6.)
5. FOR ALL Approved Briefs, the number of distinct Source_Links in the Markdown_Export SHALL equal the number of distinct Source_Links in the Brief.
6. IF the Markdown_Export fails to generate or download, THEN THE Copilot SHALL show an error message indicating that the export failed, SHALL keep the Brief Approved and unchanged, and SHALL allow the SE to retry the export.

### Requirement 9: Mask PII

**User Story:** As a team lead, I want PII such as email addresses masked in model input and output, so that personal data is not sent to or returned by the model in clear form.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Copilot submits an Amazon Bedrock request, THE PII_Masker SHALL replace every email address and every other PII type that the PII_Masker is configured to detect in the request input with a placeholder before the request is sent to the model, so that 0 detected PII values appear in clear form in the sent input.
2. WHEN Amazon Bedrock returns a response, THE PII_Masker SHALL replace every email address and every other PII type that the PII_Masker is configured to detect in the response output with a placeholder before the response is passed to the Copilot, so that 0 detected PII values appear in clear form in the returned output.
3. THE Copilot SHALL send 100% of Amazon Bedrock requests through the Model_Gateway, with the PII_Masker applied, and SHALL have no code path that calls Amazon Bedrock without the PII_Masker. (See Ambiguity A7.)
4. IF the PII_Masker fails to process a request input or a response output, THEN THE Model_Gateway SHALL NOT send the request or pass the response on, SHALL return an error to the caller indicating that PII masking failed, and SHALL NOT include the unmasked content in that error.

### Requirement 10: Return partial results when a research step fails

**User Story:** As an SE, I want a brief even when one research step fails, so that I still get the parts that worked.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. IF exactly one research step fails, THEN THE Copilot SHALL return the Brief with every Section that does not depend on the failed step completed and unchanged by the failure.
2. IF one research step fails, THEN THE Copilot SHALL mark each Section that depends on that step as a Flagged_Section and SHALL include in that Section a statement that names the failed step and says the content is unavailable.
3. IF one research step fails, THEN THE Copilot SHALL NOT present content from the failed step in a Flagged_Section as if it were complete.
4. WHEN a Section is a Flagged_Section, THE Copilot SHALL display a flag indicator on that Section, visible without expanding or scrolling within the Section, that distinguishes it from unflagged Sections. (See Ambiguity A8 for the Sections that depend on each step.)
5. IF every research step fails, THEN THE Copilot SHALL return a Brief in which every dependent Section is a Flagged_Section and SHALL tell the user that no research content could be produced.

### Requirement 11: Meet time and rate limits

**User Story:** As an SE, I want the draft brief in minutes, so that call preparation no longer takes hours.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the SE submits an Invite, THE Copilot SHALL return the full Draft Brief within 120 seconds of submission, measured from the moment the Invite is submitted to the moment the complete Draft Brief is displayed. (See Ambiguity A9.)
2. THE Model_Gateway SHALL send no more than one Amazon Bedrock request in any one-second window, queuing any further requests until the window has passed.
3. THE Copilot SHALL send every Amazon Bedrock request to the us-east-1 region.
4. WHILE a Draft Brief is being produced, THE Copilot SHALL display a progress indicator that is visible within 2 seconds of Invite submission and remains visible until the Draft Brief is displayed or an error is shown.
5. IF the Draft Brief is not complete 120 seconds after the Invite is submitted, THEN THE Copilot SHALL stop waiting, show an error message indicating that the time limit was exceeded, and keep the submitted Invite so the SE can resubmit it without re-entering it.
6. IF an Amazon Bedrock request is rejected for exceeding a rate or quota limit, THEN THE Model_Gateway SHALL retry the request at most 3 times, waiting at least 1 second between attempts, and SHALL show an error message indicating the rate limit if all 3 retries fail.

### Requirement 12: Run locally and demonstrate the full flow

**User Story:** As a team lead, I want the flow to run locally on a company chosen at runtime, so that the team can demonstrate it in three minutes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Copilot SHALL run on a single local machine, started by one documented command, with no cloud deployment required.
2. WHERE time allows, THE Copilot SHALL be deployable to AWS in us-east-1 and SHALL NOT use any other AWS Region.
3. WHEN an Invite for a public company chosen at runtime is pasted, THE Copilot SHALL let the user complete the sequence of producing the Brief, editing one line of the Brief, approving the Brief and downloading the Markdown_Export, with no setup steps between pasting the Invite and downloading the Markdown_Export.
4. WHEN the user completes the sequence in criterion 3 on a local machine with network access, THE Copilot SHALL finish the sequence, from pasting the Invite to the Markdown_Export download starting, within 180 seconds, excluding the time the user takes to edit the line.
5. IF the network is unavailable at demonstration time, THEN THE Copilot SHALL run the sequence in criterion 3 from a cached run of a previously produced Brief, and SHALL show a visible indication that the run is cached. (See Ambiguity A10.)
6. IF a required step in the sequence fails during the demonstration, THEN THE Copilot SHALL show an error message indicating which step failed and SHALL keep the Invite text and any Brief already produced.

## Ambiguities and Open Questions

The brief does not settle the points below. Each is stated as found, with no assumption built into a requirement beyond what is noted. A person should decide before the design is approved.

- **A1. Unusable invite.** The brief does not say what happens when the Invite names no company, names several, or cannot be parsed. The draft plan infers the company from attendee email domains. Requirement 1.6 covers only the no-company case and rests on the brief's rule that the Copilot does not guess. Needs a decision, especially because email addresses are masked (A7).
- **A2. Size tier boundaries.** The brief lists four tiers but no thresholds. The architecture document proposes employee counts (enterprise above 5,000, mid-market 500 to 5,000, SMB 50 to 500, boutique below 50) and says they are to be confirmed. The plan also mentions revenue. The brief does not say what to show when size cannot be found with a source. Requirements 2.2 and 3.1 conflict in that case: the tier would have no source, so it would be omitted, yet every Brief must have one.
- **A3. Real-attendee search.** The brief says attendee search uses public information only, and the mentor is confirming at kickoff whether this conflicts with the one-pager's "test data only" line. The workspace rule requires synthetic data only for any real business, customer or personal data. Requirement 4 stays as written, and tests and fixtures must use synthetic attendees. The mentor's answer may change Requirement 4.
- **A4. Number of case studies and BU list.** The success criteria say three matching case studies, but Requirement 5 in the brief gives no count. The draft plan says exactly three when at least three are available. The brief does not say what to show with fewer than three. The BU list is decided after the scrape, so no BU values are fixed here. How the SE chooses a BU in the user interface is not specified beyond "optional".
- **A5. Re-opening an approved brief.** The brief says an approved brief cannot be edited. It does not say whether the SE can unlock it or regenerate a new Draft. Requirement 7.4 records the stricter reading. Needs confirmation.
- **A6. What the export contains.** The brief says the export includes every source link, but does not say whether it contains the SE's edited text or the original. Requirement 8.4 assumes edited text. The architecture document gives a Markdown layout, which is not copied here. Needs confirmation.
- **A7. PII masking.** The brief marks the masking decision as awaiting the mentor's confirmation. It does not list the PII types beyond "such as email addresses", or say whether masked values are restored in the displayed Brief. Masking email addresses before the model sees them may weaken any email-domain inference from A1.
- **A8. Failure scope.** The brief says "one research step" can fail but does not define the steps. The draft plan names company research, attendee verification and case study matching. The mapping from step to Section is not defined, and neither is behavior when more than one step fails or when every step fails.
- **A9. Timing.** The brief says "under about 2 minutes". Requirement 11.1 uses 120 seconds as a testable figure. The brief does not say how many attendees this assumes. The one-request-per-second limit makes the time grow with attendee count. The "hours saved per call" figure is still to be supplied by Sravani and is not a requirement.
- **A10. Demo fallback.** The brief names a backup recording or cached run as a success criterion. A recording is not a Copilot behavior. A cached run could be a Copilot feature, and the brief does not say which is intended.
- **A11. Technology stack conflict.** The draft plan and architecture use Python with FastAPI, Strands Agents and Next.js, with no database. The repository steering files describe a React 19 and Vite single-page app with no backend, and the product steering says the app stays client-side unless asked otherwise. The brief names no technology. This is a design decision, so no requirement depends on it, but it must be settled before design.
- **A12. Brief storage.** The brief does not say whether Briefs persist across restarts. The draft plan holds them in memory or a local JSON file. The edit-history requirement (6.3) needs at least storage for the life of the run.
- **A13. Search tool terms and cost.** The draft plan notes that the web search tool's terms require citations to be kept and shown, and forbid bulk storage of results or building an index from them. This is a constraint on design. Requirement 2.3 and 5.5 reflect it. Post-free-tier pricing is unconfirmed and no requirement depends on it.
- **A14. Access control.** The one-pager mentions a BU filter with two demo users if time allows. The brief puts full role-based access control out of scope and lists no user accounts. Requirements assume a single user with no sign-in.
- **A15. Added during detailing.** Requirement 3 now uses the architecture document's employee-count thresholds, with SMB set to 50 to 499 because the proposal overlaps at 50 and 500, and a "size not found" outcome that resolves the conflict in A2. Both need a person's confirmation. Requirement 1 sets a 10,000-character Invite limit, Requirement 4 a 10-second attendee search timeout, Requirement 8 a 5-second export time, Requirement 11 a retry limit of 3, and Requirement 12 a 180-second demo sequence. These figures are not in the brief and need confirmation. Requirement 10 assumes a Section that depends on several steps is flagged when any one fails, and sets no retry before flagging. A8 must be settled before Requirement 10 is testable.
